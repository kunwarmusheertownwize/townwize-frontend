Verification

Checked on the person, not the account

On most ride apps the background check belongs to the account holder — the person actually driving can be anyone they handed the keys to. Townwize verifies the human behind the wheel, every time.

Fares

A fare you can check line by line

Base fare, distance, time and a competitive platform fee — each shown separately, before you book and again on your receipt. If the route changed along the way, the receipt shows the adjustment instead of burying it.

Wizers keep more of every fare than the 20–25% commission platforms allow, which is how fares stay lower for Towners at the same time.

Questions

Before you join

When are you launching?

Early Access is invite-only, and invitations go out in waves, in the order people joined the waitlist. Join now and you will hear from us as soon as your turn comes up.

I am not a BTK resident — can I still ride?

Yes, as a Guest Towner, if an already-verified Towner invites you. You are vouched for inside a resident's network, so you can start with basic details. Wizers must be verified BTK residents — no exceptions.

What if no Wizer is available near me?

We build the Wizer side first so there are cars on the road when Towners arrive, and the app shows honest pickup estimates rather than an optimistic one.

What do I need to register as a Wizer?

Six things: BTK ID or proof of residence, a utility bill at that address, your CNIC, a valid driving licence, vehicle registration, and a live selfie. The checks run automatically as you upload.

How is this different from other ride apps?

Verified identity. Elsewhere the background check belongs to the account holder, so the person driving may be an unverified stranger. Here every Wizer is a verified BTK resident. And a competitive commission instead of 20–25%, so Wizers earn more and fares stay lower.
